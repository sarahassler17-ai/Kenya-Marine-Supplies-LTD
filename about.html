Who We Are

Kenya Marine Supplies Ltd is a Kenyan company established to provide boats, marine transport services, and aquatic equipment to customers across Kenya. Our aim is to support fishermen, boat operators, tourism businesses, shipping companies, and other customers who depend on water transport and marine activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality boats, reliable marine transport services, and dependable marine equipment while maintaining customer satisfaction and promoting safety in marine operations.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted marine supplies and transport service provider in Kenya and across the East African region.

Our Operations in Kenya

We serve customers along the Kenyan coastline and around Lake Victoria. Our activities include supplying fishing boats and passenger boats, offering boat hire and marine transport services, and providing essential equipment such as life jackets, anchors, fishing nets, and boat engines.

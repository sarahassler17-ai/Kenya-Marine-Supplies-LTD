1. Boats for Sale

We offer different types of boats to meet the needs of fishermen, passengers, tourism businesses, and boat operators. Our range includes fishing boats, passenger boats, and speedboats.

Fishing Boats

Fishing boats support fishing activities in lakes and coastal waters, helping fishermen carry out their daily work.

Passenger Boats and Speedboats

Passenger boats transport people across water, while speedboats are suitable for faster journeys and recreational activities.

2. Marine Transport Services

We provide marine transport solutions for customers who need to travel or move goods by water. Our services include passenger transport, cargo transport, and boat hire.

Our transport services are intended to support boat operators, tourism businesses, and customers involved in marine activities.

3. Marine and Aquatic Equipment

We supply essential marine equipment to support safe and efficient boat operations.

  • Life jackets: Help keep passengers and crew afloat if they fall into the water.
  • Anchors: Hold a boat in place when it is stopped and help prevent it from drifting.
  • Fishing nets: Used to catch fish for commercial or subsistence fishing.
  • Navigation equipment: Compasses, charts, and GPS devices help boat operators plan routes and know their position.
  • Boat engines: Provide power to move a boat through the water.
  • Ropes: Used to tie boats to docks, secure loads, and assist with mooring.
  • Fire extinguishers: Help control small fires onboard and are an important safety item.
